Detailed Description

Parser of the NMEA 0183 sentences of a GNSS receiver that carry the time and the state of the fix: RMC and ZDA (date and time), GGA (fix quality, satellites used) and GSV (satellites in view). Any talker (GP, GN, GL, GA, GB, ...) is accepted; sentences with a wrong checksum are ignored. It needs no hardware, so it can be fed from any source.
